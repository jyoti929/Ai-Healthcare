Health & Wellness Tips

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration improves immunity, brain function and body energy.

Healthy Food

Eat fruits, vegetables and protein-rich food. Avoid junk food and maintain balanced nutrition.

Daily Exercise

Do at least 30 minutes of walking, exercise or stretching daily to improve overall health.

Yoga & Meditation

Meditation and yoga reduce stress, improve breathing and increase mental peace.

Fitness Routine

Maintain a consistent workout routine for better stamina and stronger immunity.

Proper Sleep

Take 7-8 hours of quality sleep daily to refresh your body and mind.
